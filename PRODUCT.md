# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Vietnamese developer learners using a personal roadmap, weekly schedule, task list, and notes to manage focused study around work and internship time.

## Product Purpose

This app helps the user plan learning phases, track completion, schedule study blocks, capture notes, and keep weekly progress visible in one place.

## Brand Personality

Focused, technical, direct. The product should feel like a compact command center for study discipline, not a marketing page.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative landing-page patterns, playful novelty UI, low-contrast gray text, and heavy visual effects that slow down task work.

## Design Principles

Make progress visible at all times.
Keep controls predictable and close to the data they affect.
Favor dense but readable layouts for repeated daily use.
Use accent color for state, selection, and priority, not decoration.
Preserve Vietnamese labels where they support the user's workflow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target readable contrast, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus states, and reduced-motion-safe transitions.
